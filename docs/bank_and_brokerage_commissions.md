# Bank of Georgia & TBC Bank Trading Commissions and Fees

This document outlines the standard trading commissions, transaction fees, and custody costs associated with retail investing platforms provided by Bank of Georgia (BOG) and TBC Bank (TBC Capital).

---

## 1. Bank of Georgia (BOG) — Mobile Bank Investing

Bank of Georgia provides streamlined retail access to international stocks and ETFs directly through its mobile banking application. The fee structure scales primarily with portfolio size.

### Trading & Transaction Fees
*   **Portfolios up to $1,000:** **$0 (Zero Commission)** on standard trades (excludes penny stocks).
*   **Portfolios above $1,000:**
    *   **Transaction Fee:** $0.3\%$ of the transaction value.
    *   **Minimum Fee:** $\$0.50$ per trade.
    *   **Maximum Fee:** $\$40.00$ per trade.

### Maintenance & Custody Fees
*   **Monthly Portfolio Maintenance:** $0.01\%$ of the existing portfolio value per month (with a minimum of $\$0.50$) applicable to accounts exceeding the $\$1,000$ tier.

## 2. TBC Bank / TBC Capital

TBC Capital provides broader market access across domestic and international exchanges (including US and European markets) via professional platforms and brokerage accounts. 

### TBC Bank Mobile App (in-app investing)
*   **Trading:** **$0** on US stocks. The app's own listing says "$0 fees for trading, account opening, deposits, and withdrawals".
*   This is the fee Mikeronn fills in for a TBC trade (`app/fees.py`). The rates below are for TBC Capital's brokerage platform, a different product.

### Trading Commissions by Asset Class / Region
*   **US Equities / Stocks:** 
    *   Typically structured per share or subject to a flat minimum per order.
    *   Standard international tariffs average around $\$0.02$ per share with a **minimum of $\$4.00$ per trade**.
*   **UK & European Equities:** 
    *   Approximately $0.1\%$ of the transaction amount.
    *   **Minimum fee:** £8.00 (or equivalent currency minimums for European exchanges).
*   **Local Georgian Securities & Bonds:** 
    *   Competitive local brokerage rates, often featuring reduced transaction fees depending on market maker participation and specific debt/equity instruments.

### Custody & Depository Fees
*   **Foreign Securities Custody:** Generally ranges from $0.12\%$ to $0.25\%$ of the total market value of the foreign investment portfolio per year, charged periodically or monthly.
